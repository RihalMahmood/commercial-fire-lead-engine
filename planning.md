## 1. Core Business Model & The "Demand Paradox"

### 1.1 The Role
We are cold email specialists and database intelligence architects. We do not inspect pipes or turn wrenches; we identify commercial properties facing active municipal fire violations, red-tags, and compliance deadlines, and we book confirmed walkthrough appointments directly onto the calendars of mid-sized fire protection contractors.

### 1.2 The "Demand Paradox"
* **The Reality in the Trade:** Fire protection is **mandated by law** (NFPA 13, 25, 72, California Title 19, and municipal fire codes). Commercial properties cannot legally operate without certified life-safety systems.
* **The Contractor's Pain:** Contractors are **not** starving for raw leads. They are overwhelmed and suffering from a severe **technician shortage**. They are drowning in low-margin (<10%) new construction bids through General Contractors (GCs) where payment is delayed 60 to 90 days.
* **The Golden Goose (What Contractors Will Pay For):** **Direct-to-owner, high-margin (50% to 70%) compliance repairs, fire marshal citation abatements, and emergency deficiency jobs.** In these scenarios, the building owner has a strict 14-to-30-day legal deadline, cannot shop around for 3 bids, deals directly with the contractor, and pays immediately via credit card or Net-15.
* **Our Offer to Contractors:** Pay-Per-Appointment ($250–$400 per completed commercial walkthrough) or Hybrid ($1,500–$2,500/month base + $150–$200/appt), backed by a free 1–2 appointment pilot.

Every lead we target must fall into one of these 4 high-urgency compliance triggers:

### 1. Intent 1: Active Impairment & Emergency Fire Watch
* **Trigger:** Sprinkler riser burst, broken main control valve, or offline alarm panel.
* **Owner's Pain:** Legally mandated to pay human guards $50–$150/hr for 24/7 hall patrols (**$1,500 to $3,500/day bleed**).
* **Target Services:** Emergency on-call repair dispatch, valve replacement, panel restoration, official Fire Marshal fire watch cancellation certification.
* **Timeframe Window:** **Past 7 to 14 days** (maximum 30 days active).

### 2. Intent 2: Municipal Notice of Violation (NOV) & Red-Tags
* **Trigger:** City Fire Marshal (LAFD) or LADBS issued an official citation or physical red-tag.
* **Owner's Pain:** Mandatory **15-to-30-day legal deadline** before compounding daily fines ($250–$1,000/day), court summons, or revocation of the Certificate of Occupancy.
* **Target Services:** Code deficiency remediation, backflow preventer rebuild/test, 5-year internal pipe inspection (Title 19 / NFPA 25), official city proof of abatement filing.
* **Timeframe Window:** **Past 30 to 60 days** (active/unabated window).

### 3. Intent 3: Overdue Compliance Portals & Chief’s Reg 4 Testing
* **Trigger:** Lapsed annual testing deadline on third-party compliance tracking platforms (*The Compliance Engine / Brycer*) or non-compliance on LAFD Chief's Regulation 4 (Reg 4) testing roster (15 mandatory life-safety systems).
* **Owner's Pain:** Automated warning escalation letters and impending formal city citations.
* **Target Services:** Complete annual NFPA 25 (sprinkler) and NFPA 72 (alarm) testing, direct portal upload to clear city flags, multi-year recurring service agreements.
* **Timeframe Window:** **Past 30 to 90 days overdue**.

### 4. Intent 4: Change of Occupancy & Commercial Real Estate Alterations
* **Trigger:** Commercial space leased, sold, or remodeled (e.g., retail converted to gym, standard warehouse converted to high-pile racking). Existing NFPA 13 sprinkler hydraulic calculations are legally void.
* **Owner/Tenant Pain:** City will **NOT** issue the Certificate of Occupancy (CofO). Tenant cannot open doors and landlord cannot collect rent.
* **Target Services:** NFPA 13 hydraulic recalculations, tenant improvement (TI) sprinkler drops and head relocations, ESFR high-pile racking upgrades, final city walkthrough sign-off.
* **Timeframe Window:** **Past 30 to 90 days** (active permit / CofO pending).

## 4. Data Ingestion & Deep-Link Permalinks (Zero Paywalls)

### 4.1 SODA API Extraction Standards
The City of Los Angeles open-data portal (`data.lacity.org`) uses the Socrata Open Data API (SODA).
* **Base URL:** `https://data.lacity.org/resource/{endpoint_id}.json`
* **Unbounded Pagination Protocol:** The scraper must loop using `$limit=1000` and `$offset=N` until zero records are returned. **No arbitrary truncations or early break statements.**
* **Temporal Query Parameter:** `$where={date_field} >= '{YYYY-MM-DD}'` using ISO 8601 timestamps.
* **Strict Rule on Ingestion:** **Do NOT filter by fire keywords in the initial API query.** Public city records dump *all* violations (trash, zoning, signs, fire) into one table. Filtering too early misses edge cases. Pull the complete raw dataset for the timeframe first.

### 4.2 Exact Deep-Link Permalinks (Direct Proof for Every Lead)
Every single row extracted must contain a direct, clickable verification URL proving the exact case to the contractor:
* **For LADBS Code Enforcement Cases (`u82d-eh7z`):**
  `https://www.ladbs.org/services/check-status/online-building-records?case_number={case_number}`
* **For LADBS Building Permits & CofO (`pi9x-tg5x`, `3f9m-afei`):**
  `https://www.ladbs.org/services/check-status/online-building-records?permit_number={permit_nbr}`
* **For SODA Direct Record Verification:**
  `https://data.lacity.org/resource/{endpoint_id}.json?case_number={case_number}`

## 7. Contact Enrichment & Deliverability Stack (BatchData + Floqer + Verisium)

### 7.1 BatchData API (Property Intelligence & LLC Piercing)
* **API Endpoint:** `https://api.batchdata.com/api/v1/property/skip-trace`
* **Function:** Takes `property_address` and `landlord_entity.company_name`.
* **Action:** Pierces opaque LLCs to find real human managing members, general partners, residential addresses, personal mobile numbers, and personal emails.

### 7.2 Floqer API (B2B Corporate People Intelligence)
* **Function:** Takes `tenant_entity.business_name` or `landlord_entity.company_name`.
* **Target Titles:** *"Director of Facilities"*, *"Chief Engineer"*, *"Property Manager"*, *"General Manager"*, *"Director of Operations"*.
* **Action:** Retrieves direct corporate work emails (`name@company.com`) and LinkedIn profile URLs.

### 7.3 Verisium (Real-Time SMTP Deliverability Verification)
* **Strict Quality Rule:** Both BatchData and Verisium extract emails, but **ALL emails must pass Verisium verification**.
* **Deliverability Logic:**
  * `Valid` (Deliverable, mailbox exists) ──► **APPROVED FOR OUTREACH**
  * `Risky` / `Catch-All` ──► **HOLD FOR MANUAL REVIEW**
  * `Invalid` / `Undeliverable` ──► **AUTOMATICALLY DROPPED**

## 8. Data Governance: Master Central Sent Suppression Database

* **Location:** Hosted in Supabase Postgres table `central_sent_suppression_database`.
* **Pre-Send Deduplication:** Before any lead or contact is transmitted to PlusVibe, the pipeline queries:
  ```sql
  SELECT id FROM central_sent_suppression_database 
  WHERE property_address = :address 
     OR recipient_email = :email 
     AND sent_timestamp >= NOW() - INTERVAL '90 days';
  ```
* **Cooldown Policy:** If found within the 90-to-180-day window, the lead is set to `SUPPRESSED` and archived immediately to prevent spam complaints and brand damage.
